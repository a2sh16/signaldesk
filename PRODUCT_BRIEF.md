## The Market Problem

The U.S. distressed property market processes over **$300 billion in transactions annually**. Wholesale investors, fix-and-flip operators, and acquisition teams depend on identifying motivated sellers before competing buyers do. The window between a distress signal appearing and a deal being locked is typically 48–72 hours.

The current workflow is broken. An analyst must manually cross-reference foreclosure filings, tax lien databases, USPS vacancy flags, probate records, and MLS comparables — across 12 separate data sources — before they can even decide whether a property is worth pursuing. This takes 4–6 hours per property. Most deals are gone before the analysis is finished.

Existing platforms like PropStream and BatchLeads sell raw data subscriptions. They surface lists of properties. They do not tell you which ones to act on, why, or how.

---

## The Insight

The difference between a good deal and a bad one is not access to data — everyone has access to data. The difference is the **speed and quality of the decision made from that data**.

Every distressed property leaves a predictable signal trail. Foreclosure filings, tax delinquency, utility shutoffs, divorce proceedings, and code violations do not appear randomly — they cluster in patterns that precede motivated seller behavior. A model trained on these patterns can score any property's probability of being a high-value acquisition target before a human analyst ever looks at it.

---

## The Solution

SignalDesk is a **decision intelligence platform** for real estate investors. It monitors 3,200+ counties continuously, scores every distressed property 1–100 using a proprietary 47-factor model, and generates a complete AI investment brief — including ARV estimate, rehab cost range, and exit strategy analysis — in under 90 seconds.

The platform replaces an entire research workflow. Investors stop spending hours on analysis and start spending minutes on decisions.

---

## Target User

**Primary:** Wholesale real estate investors running 5–50 deals per month who currently use PropStream, BatchLeads, or manual county research. They have an existing acquisition process but are bottlenecked by lead qualification time.

**Secondary:** Fix-and-flip operators and acquisition managers at mid-size investment firms who need to scale deal volume without proportionally scaling headcount.
