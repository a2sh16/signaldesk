# SignalDesk — 5-Minute Demo Script

> **For Loom recordings, investor calls, and partner presentations.**
> Follow this script in order. Total runtime: 4:45–5:15 minutes.

---

## Before You Start

- Open [signaldeskai-dev.manus.space](https://signaldeskai-dev.manus.space) in a clean browser window
- Set browser zoom to 90% for better visibility
- Use a 1920×1080 window or full-screen Loom capture
- Sign in to your account before recording
- **Do NOT show:** your email address, any real API keys, the Settings panel, or the `/dashboard` URL bar

---

## Script

### 0:00 — Opening (15 seconds)

> "This is SignalDesk — the autonomous acquisition desk for real estate investors. Instead of spending 4 to 6 hours researching a property, you get a scored, analyzed, brief-ready decision in 90 seconds. Let me show you how."

**Action:** Start on the homepage hero. Let the live ticker animate for 5 seconds.

---

### 0:15 — The Problem (30 seconds)

> "Every real estate investor has the same problem. PropStream gives you data. BatchLeads gives you lists. DealMachine gives you driving routes. But none of them give you a decision. You still have to manually pull comps, estimate rehab, model exits, and figure out which leads to call first. SignalDesk replaces that entire workflow."

**Action:** Scroll slowly down to the "How It Works" section. Pause on the 3-step diagram.

---

### 0:45 — The Deal Score (45 seconds)

> "Every property in SignalDesk gets a Deal Score from 1 to 100. This score is calculated from 47 factors across 5 dimensions: equity depth, distress intensity, market velocity, owner motivation, and data confidence. A score above 85 means a highly motivated seller with strong equity — exactly who you want to call first."

**Action:** Scroll to the Deal Score section. Point to the 91/100 gauge on screen. Pause 3 seconds.

> "And the score isn't just a number. It tells you exactly why a property scored the way it did — which signals fired, what the equity position looks like, and what the risk flags are."

---

### 1:30 — The AI Investment Brief (60 seconds)

> "Once you find a high-scoring property, you click Generate Brief. In 90 seconds, SignalDesk produces a complete investment memo — ARV using a 3-tier methodology, rehab cost estimate, 3 exit strategies with projected returns, and a risk flag summary. You can export it as a PDF and send it to your buyer in minutes."

**Action:** Scroll to the AI Brief mockup section. Point to the brief card.

> "Our ARV engine achieves within 5% accuracy on 87% of properties in markets with sufficient comparable sales data. Every estimate includes a confidence score and source breakdown. And if we ever get it wrong, there's a 1-click Autopsy button — we review within 24 hours and refund your credit. No questions asked."

---

### 2:30 — Live Deal Feed (30 seconds)

> "The Live Deal Feed shows you the highest-scoring properties in your target counties, updated every 24 hours. You can filter by signal type, score range, ARV, and equity. Your acquisition desk never sleeps."

**Action:** Scroll to the Live Deal Feed section. Let the deal cards animate into view.

---

### 3:00 — Comparison (30 seconds)

> "Here's how we stack up against the tools you're probably already using."

**Action:** Navigate to `/compare`. Let the comparison table load.

> "SignalDesk is the only platform with AI deal scoring, AI investment briefs, exit strategy modeling, a bad data guarantee, and an AppSumo lifetime deal. PropStream and BatchLeads don't have any of these."

---

### 3:30 — Pricing (30 seconds)

> "Pricing starts at $99 a month for the Analyst plan — 30 AI briefs, deal score on all properties, ARV engine, Chrome Extension, and PDF export. The Closer plan at $199 adds skip trace, direct mail, and Zapier. And right now, we have an AppSumo lifetime deal — $129 one time for the Closer plan forever."

**Action:** Navigate to `/pricing`. Show the three plan cards and the AppSumo banner.

---

### 4:00 — Sign In Flow (30 seconds)

> "Signing up takes 30 seconds. No password required — we email you a 6-digit code. You can also sign in with Google."

**Action:** Navigate to `/signin`. Show the email input and the Google button. Do NOT actually sign in during the recording.

---

### 4:30 — Close (30 seconds)

> "SignalDesk is live today at signaldeskai-dev.manus.space. 14-day free trial, no credit card required. If you're serious about finding and closing more deals, this is the platform that replaces your entire research workflow."

**Action:** Return to the homepage hero. End recording.

---

## What NOT to Show

| Do Not Show | Reason |
|---|---|
| Your email address | Privacy |
| The `/dashboard` URL or any user account data | Privacy |
| Settings panel or any admin routes | Exposes internal structure |
| Any real API keys or environment variables | Security |
| The Stripe test card number | Avoid confusion |
| Any error states or loading spinners | Professionalism |
| The "Made with Manus" badge | Competitive positioning |
